Best Investment Advice

The smartest investment advice is simple: don't leave. As Peter Lynch observed, investors have lost far more money bracing for crashes than the crashes themselves ever took.

Over time it becomes obvious that trading too much is futile. The urge to react — to buy on optimism, sell on fear — feels like smart decision-making. In reality, for anyone who isn't a generational trading savant, every clever move chips away at long-term returns.

Think about everything the world has thrown at us over the past seven years. And still, the US market has soared, minting wealth at a staggering pace. Look at the Forbes list — those names aren't traders. They're founders who held on. You just have to stay invested along these great founders.

On Homeschooling

Given the school-age children population of 50 million in the US, how many kids would you guess are homeschooled in the US? I would certainly guess a number below a million. 3-4 million kids are apparently homeschooled in the US. This is probably because it is legal there and an industry has established itself around it.

My guess is many people are skeptical of homeschooling. I was too. Yet after becoming a dad, I started asking myself if homeschooling was actually superior.

We do not actively teach our 5 year old anything academic. Due to natural curiosity she learned the Cyrillic letters and can read basic words. Obviously there are much more capable 5 year olds out there, my point is if a kid can learn basic stuff without active teaching - imagine the things she could learn at a 1-2 hour/day pace with a teacher. I believe 2 hours a day is enough to master the current school curriculum. Evidence for that is Alpha School in the US, which has 2-hour academic sessions a day, and the rest of the day spent doing fun stuff. Alpha School students score very high in state exams compared to peers.

The benefits of homeschooling are twofold in my mind: 1) the pacing of academic material is tailored to your kid's abilities, 2) it frees up time for your kid to do other stuff (playing, extracurricular activities etc).

To be clear, we are not planning to homeschool. Homeschooling is a very serious undertaking that requires strong commitment from parents. I am not sure if we are ready for such a commitment. Frankly, I am not sure if it's legal in Kazakhstan. Just wanted to highlight that we don't discuss homeschooling enough as a society.
